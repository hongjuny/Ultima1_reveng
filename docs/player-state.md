# Player record: observed persistence through death

`GEN` loads the 458-byte `U1.VARS` record from `$6000–$61C9` and copies it to `$7E6D–$8036`. This page tracks that live memory range for one **controlled** castle death. The trace starts at the `CAS` attack-handler return, changes only the player's low HP byte `$7EC6` from `$96` to `$0A` before a known `$0F` guard hit, then follows the shared death path and the `OUT` resurrection path to the first outdoor key poll.

| Record byte | Before intervention | Selector `$88FD` | First OUT input `$8A6C` | Observed source of change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `$7E7A` | `$01` | `$01` | `$00` | `OUT` `$8A17` clears it |
| `$7E7B` | `$01` | `$01` | `$00` | `OUT` `$8A14` clears it |
| `$7E84` | `$02` | `$02` | `$00` | `OUT` `$8A0E–$8A13` clears table entries `$7E84–$7E92` |
| `$7EC6` | `$96` | `$00` | `$63` | Intervention `$96→$0A`; guard damage borrows; shared death reset clears HP; `OUT` refills it to 99 |
| `$7ED4` | `$64` | `$00` | `$00` | Shared death reset clears the two-byte `Coin` field `$7ED4/$7ED5` |
| `$7EE5` | `$C3` | `$00` | `$63` | Shared death reset clears food; `OUT` refills it to 99 |

These are **the only six changed bytes** in this record between the pre-intervention snapshot and the first resurrected input: the other 452 bytes match. At the intermediate selector snapshot, the changes are `$7EC6`, `$7ED4`, and `$7EE5`; the three `$7E7A/$7E7B/$7E84` changes occur later in `OUT`. The high bytes `$7EC7`, `$7ED5`, and `$7EE6` are zero at all three checkpoints. The comparison and snapshot ancestry are checked by [`tools/verify_resurrection_record.py`](../tools/verify_resurrection_record.py).

The shared [`MI.U1` death reset](../disasm/MI.U1-death-reset.s) at `$888D–$88A0` explicitly zeros `$7ED4/$7ED5`, `$7EE5/$7EE6`, and `$7EC6/$7EC7`. The [`OUT` setup](../disasm/OUT-resurrection-init.s) clears `$7E79–$7E7B` and a table while choosing a new position; its [`HP/food loops`](../disasm/OUT-resurrection-fill.s) raise the low bytes to `$63`. In this replay, the new outdoor coordinates were `(3,8)`.

Known field meanings: `$7EC6/$7EC7` are HP, `$7EE5/$7EE6` are food, and `$7ED4/$7ED5` are the two-byte `Coin` value. The shared status routine's [byte-matched value renderer](../disasm/MI.U1-status-values.s) reads them in the same order as its preceding Hits/Food/Exp./Coin labels. A [town food-purchase fragment](../disasm/TWN-food-purchase.s) compares this value with a two-byte cost, subtracts the cost on success, and credits food. Runtime purchase and insufficient-funds replays confirm the field's currency role, including the exact Coin 4/5 boundary for a cost of 5; see [TWN](../disasm/TWN.md). `$7E7A` contributes to attack damage, and `$7E7B` contributes to a guard's hit threshold, but their complete meanings remain open. The role of `$7E84` is also unresolved. No claim about all possible death states follows from this one replay. Future port work needs ordinary-play death traces and tests with nonzero high bytes, inventory, quests, equipment, and other state fields.

The sixth two-byte character attribute starts at `$7ED2`. The town food-shop price code reads its low byte and decreases the 5-Coin pack price in 20-point steps, down to 1. The attribute's displayed name and other uses still need confirmation. Controlled price-tier replays cover the 19/20, 39/40, 59/60, and 79/80 boundaries; see [TWN](../disasm/TWN.md).

Four adjacent live record bytes `$7E75–$7E78` are castle gem-award counters in red, green, blue, white order. `CAS` increments them at `$963A,$9697,$96F0,$9755`; `OUT` tests all four before selecting `TM` at `$8D73–$8D8A`. The award texts and opcodes identify their roles statically, while a controlled B-key replay confirms the nonzero gate and TM load. The actual quest actions that increment each counter and the effect of repeated awards still need runtime traces; see [CAS](../disasm/CAS.md) and [turn contracts](turn-contracts.md).

The player record also persists a real outdoor turn. `OUT`'s `Q` command saves the same 458-byte `$7E6D–$8036` span as GEN. After one Space turn, the file differed from GEN's initial save only at `$7EDA:00→50` and `$7EE4:95→90`. GEN `B` reloaded the changed file and restored all 458 bytes to live memory before selecting `OUT`. See the [record format](../disasm/U1.VARS.md) and [round-trip verifier](../tools/verify_out_player_save_reload.py). These two bytes are observed shared turn state; their complete units and behavior across other commands still need classification.

The eight quest-state bytes `$7EA9–$7EB0` follow `0` (available), `1` (accepted), `$FF` (completed) in the statically checked castle, outdoor, and dungeon paths. Castle reporting resets a completed byte to `0`. Even indices complete at outdoor landmarks; odd indices complete after a dungeon type-5 kill at matching depths. `$7EB5` is a one-shot final-craft creation request: `CAS` can set it and `OUT` clears it after attempting to place raw tile `$1E`. A controlled CAS→OUT replay confirmed the world-map write and clear; see the [quest and craft map](quest-and-final-craft.md). `$7EA8` limits that creation path but its broader meaning remains unknown.

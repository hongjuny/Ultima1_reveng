# First dungeon map: observed memory format

This page describes the first dungeon reached from outdoor tile `(43,15)` in this disk version. The private replays and byte comparisons are checked by [`verify_dng_generation.py`](../tools/verify_dng_generation.py) and [`verify_dng_directions.py`](../tools/verify_dng_directions.py). The generator's rules for other entrances and levels remain open.

## Storage and indexing

`DNG` ends at `$B098`. Its entry code copies a 121-byte template from file-backed `$A619–$A691` into writable `$B099–$B111`. The [map reader](../disasm/DNG-map-access.s) at `$9D32` and [writer](../disasm/DNG-map-write.s) at `$A41F` both use the address formula `$B099 + 11*y + x`. Each source fragment reassembles to the exact extracted bytes. The runtime map is therefore an 11×11 row-major array for this path; `$B099–$B111` is generated state beyond the `DNG` file payload.

At `$8C87`, the entire working array matches the template. At `$8CBB`, 35 cells differ from the template. By the first main-loop entry `$8DE6`, another 10 cells differ from the `$8CBB` state. The resulting map is unchanged at the first key poll. In this observed map, all 40 border cells are `$01`, and the starting `(1,1)` cell is `$08`. The complete array remains in ignored private snapshots, not this public repository.

## First generator seed

The `$8C5D` entry code initializes two state bytes before generating the map:

```text
$A504 = (($7EED << 3) XOR outdoor_x[$7E73] XOR $A506) & $FF
$A505 = ((outdoor_y[$7E74] << 2) XOR $7E6F) & $FF
```

For the observed entry, `$7EED=$0B`, outdoor coordinates are `(43,15)`, `$7E6F=0`, and `$A506=1`. The seed is `$72,$3C` after template copy. It evolves to `$A7,$C4` at `$8CBB` and `$01,$28` at `$8DE6`. The meanings of `$7EED` and `$7E6F`, and the complete random sequence, still need tracing. These observations establish one concrete seed and map, not a general generator specification.

The same stages were captured independently on the 1→2 descent, 2→3 descent, and 2→1 return. At `$8C87`, all three working maps equal the same 121-byte `$A619` template. The seed formula above gives `$71,$3C` for depth 2, `$70,$3C` for depth 3, and `$72,$3C` on return to depth 1; all three were observed at the copy-complete checkpoint. Each continued through `$8CBB` to a distinct generated map at `$8DE6`, and the `$8DDA` poll retained that map. The private [`verify_dng_generation_levels.py`](../tools/verify_dng_generation_levels.py) checks each phase and matches the final full RAM against the independent stair replay. This establishes the seed formula and template copy for these three transitions; the internal random and layout routines still need a complete specification.

The [99-byte stair-placement fragment](../disasm/DNG-stair-placement.s) at `$8D36–$8D98` reassembles exactly. Its depth-parity branch places `$07` at `(7,3)` and `$08` at `(3,7)` on even levels, reversing them on odd levels. The depth-1 override puts `$08` at `(1,1)` and clears `(7,3)` to `$00`. These cells match the observed depth-1, depth-2, and depth-3 maps. The fragment also clears `(7,3)` at depth 10; that branch is identified in code but has not been exercised at runtime.

The returned depth-1 map has the same initialized seed as the first depth-1 map, but `(2,3)` changes `$05→$06`. A targeted replay stopped immediately after the shared `$15B9` call at `$8D20`. The first entry returned `$40` and wrote `$05` at `(2,3)`; the return entry returned `$6F` and wrote `$06` there. The [17-byte random-cell fragment](../disasm/DNG-random-cell.s) reassembles exactly and computes `5 + (random_byte & 1)`. The [private verifier](../tools/verify_dng_random_tile.py) checks both draws, written cells, and independent final RAM matches. This explains the observed low-nibble change and demonstrates that the two-byte seed alone does not determine every generated byte: this placement also consumes the shared random stream. The other map-byte differences still need attribution.

## Movement evidence

The [candidate routine](../disasm/DNG-candidate-position.s) at `$A438` adds facing `$A4EC/$A4ED` to local position `$A4E8/$A4E9`. The direction keys and Space were replayed independently from the same first poll:

| Input | Local position at next poll | Facing at next poll | Handler |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Up `$0B` | `(1,2)` | `(0,+1)` | `$8E95` |
| Down `$0A` | `(1,1)` | `(0,-1)` | `$8E48` |
| Right `$15` | `(1,1)` | `(-1,0)` | `$8E5B` |
| Left `$08` | `(1,1)` | `(+1,0)` | `$8E78` |
| Space `$20` | `(1,1)` | `(0,+1)` | `$8F8C` |

With facing `(-1,0)`, Up read `$01` at `(0,1)` and took the blocked path `$8EA7`; position stayed `(1,1)`. With facing `(+1,0)`, Up read `$00` at `(2,1)`, took `$8EBE`, and moved to `(2,1)`. HP and food stayed unchanged through the next poll in each replay. Thus `$01` blocks and `$00` admits movement in these tested cases. The meaning of other cell values, visual rendering, encounters, stairs, and per-turn costs remains open.

The array can change during the turn after movement. After advancing east to `(3,1)`, the `$98EC` turn routine changed two map cells: `(4,3)` `$33→$03` and `(3,3)` `$00→$30`. The high-nibble slot encoding below identifies this as slot 3 changing cells in the observed map. On the following input, the candidate `(4,1)` held `$13` and took the blocked path `$8EA7`. The movement handler compares the full byte, so `$13` is a separate observed blocked value; this replay does not by itself establish the behavior of bare `$03`.

The high nibble is tied to an actor slot in the observed maps. Across 16 private input snapshots spanning depths 1–3 and movement across level 2, every one of 48 occupied cells has high nibble 1–5. Its `(x,y)` matches `$A50E[slot]` and `$A514[slot]`, and `$A508[slot]` is zero. The [byte-matched spawn fragment](../disasm/DNG-actor-encode.s) shifts `$A4FE` left four times before writing it to the map. Occupied bytes such as `$13`, `$34`, and `$58` also carry a low nibble; `$58` was seen on the level-two entrance `$08`. The [private actor-map verifier](../tools/verify_dng_actor_map.py) checks all observed slots and coordinates.

The observed slot-3 move from `(4,3)` to `(3,3)` now has instruction-level evidence. At `$997F`, `PLA; AND #$0F; JSR $A41F` changed the old cell from `$33` to `$03`, preserving its low nibble. At `$9988`, the candidate X coordinate `$03` was stored in `$A50E[3]`, while Y stayed `$03`. The code read the destination cell `$00` into `$A4F2`, then `$999D–$99A9` shifted slot 3 left four times, ORed in that destination byte, and wrote `$30`. The [43-byte horizontal movement fragment](../disasm/DNG-actor-move-x.s) reassembles exactly. The [private horizontal movement verifier](../tools/verify_dng_actor_move.py) checks each intermediate write and matches the final full RAM against the independently captured next input poll.

The following ordinary turn moved the same slot vertically from `(3,3)` to `(3,2)`. The [43-byte vertical counterpart](../disasm/DNG-actor-move-y.s) cleared the departed `$30` cell to `$00` at `$99D9–$99DB`, updated `$A514[3]` from 3 to 2 at `$99E2–$99E4`, then overlaid `$30` on destination `$00` at `$99FE–$9A00`. Its [private verifier](../tools/verify_dng_actor_move_y.py) checks the intermediate writes and full RAM against another independent next-poll replay. These two turns confirm old-cell restoration and destination overlay for one actor moving on two axes. What nonzero `$A508` means and movement over other terrain codes remain open.

At starting cell `(1,1)` with value `$08` and facing `(0,+1)`, command `K` selects the ascend branch at `$9666`. In this first-depth case `$A506` changes `1→0`, then `$9716` exits to `OUT`. The outdoor coordinates `(43,15)` return unchanged. This confirms the entrance tile's exit use in one state.

An ordinary-play replay followed 17 direction inputs from `(1,1)` to the `$07` cell at `(3,7)`. Each input was checked against the previous private snapshot, including the current 11×11 grid, position, facing, depth, and HP. HP changed `150→139` during the route; this run does not assign each loss to a particular actor or hazard. A right-turn input at the stairs changed facing `(+1,0)→(0,+1)` while leaving the cell `$07` and depth 1 unchanged. With that facing, `K` entered `$961B`, passed the vertical-facing check at `$963F`, selected the non-`$08` path at `$9666`, and entered the [byte-matched depth increment](../disasm/DNG-descend-entry.s) at `$972C`. The next-level generator `$8C5D` ran before the next `$8DDA` input poll. At that poll, depth is 2, local position remains `(3,7)`, its regenerated cell is `$08`, HP is `131`, and food is `180`. The private [`verify_dng_stairs.py`](../tools/verify_dng_stairs.py) checks the complete route and handoff. The regenerated second-floor grid is kept only in the ignored snapshot; its general generation rule remains open.

From that exact level-two poll, another `K` on `$08` followed `$961B→$963F→$9666→$9670→$9728→$8C5D`. The [byte-matched `$9728` call](../disasm/DNG-ascend-regenerate.s) regenerated depth 1; at its next poll, `(3,7)` held `$07` again. HP `131` and food `180` did not change during this return. Relative to the original first-floor poll, six map bytes differ after the return; only `(2,3)` changed its low nibble, `$05→$06`. That is one observed round trip, not proof that the full floor layout is always retained. The exact meaning of the changed tile and high-nibble differences remains open.

The same `$07` stair was also tested before the final turn, while facing east `(1,0)`. `K` reached `$963F` but returned to `$8E0E` without reaching `$9666`; depth, position, facing, tile, HP, and food were unchanged at the handler return. At the next poll, depth was still 1 and HP had fallen `139→138` during the following dungeon turn. The [8-byte facing gate](../disasm/DNG-stair-facing.s) reads vertical facing `$A4ED` and branches to `$9666` only when nonzero. This verifies one horizontal refusal at `$07`; it does not test the other horizontal direction or `$08/$09`.

With the local emulator and first dungeon poll snapshot prepared, `ULTIMA_A2M_SOCKETPAIR=1 python3 tools/trace_dng_descend.py` reproduces the route, horizontal refusal, and both stair transitions. `python3 tools/verify_dng_stairs.py` checks the resulting private snapshots.

Starting from the saved level-two first poll, a second ordinary-play route reached that floor's `$07` stair at `(7,3)` in 11 direction inputs. The player approached facing north `(0,-1)` and HP fell `131→108` during the route. All five hits causing that loss were [traced separately](dungeon-combat.md). `K` passed the same `$963F` vertical-facing gate, entered `$972C`, and regenerated depth 3 through `$8C5D`. At the third-floor first poll, position remained `(7,3)`, its tile became `$08`, and HP/food remained `108/180`. This confirms that either nonzero sign of vertical facing can pass the gate in the two tested descents. The [private level-three verifier](../tools/verify_dng_level3.py) checks every snapshot in this second route. Locally, `ULTIMA_A2M_SOCKETPAIR=1 python3 tools/trace_dng_level2_to3.py` reproduces the route and combat replays after the level-two snapshot exists. The third-floor map structure and deeper rules remain open.
